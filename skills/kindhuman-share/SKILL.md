# kindhuman-share

Read the contract. Identify an available authenticated publication capability and the person's intended audience. If none exists, prepare a private preview and report the dependency.

Show the exact public words, title and selected assets before publishing. Approval to capture, upload privately, or preserve a Snapshot does not authorize publication. Check nested Snapshot contents as well as the top-level item. Do not expose originals, private source URLs, transcript bystanders, internal interpretations or rejected connections by default.

Publish only following the person's explicit decision about that preview. Verify through the public route without relying on the owner's private session. The current CLI has no publish operation; never claim that recording local approval made anything public.
