# The first session should already feel alive

Settled product decisions, 2026-09-25:

1. Support Codex, Cursor and Meta Muse Code.
2. Collect on an agent-run schedule from sources chosen by the person.
3. Keep the inbox at the user's end. No KindHuman uploads before review.
4. The person chooses cadence and timezone during setup; no default rhythm.
5. KindHuman servers hold approved Moments and Snapshots and render memory lanes for their owners and material they choose to display to others.
6. Every scheduled check-in offers a thoughtful invitation. Empty input is not an instruction to go silent.
7. Setup includes the first experience immediately, and ultimately a verified live Moment.

## Setup conversation

“What would you like KindHuman to draw from?” Offer specific available sources, including a selected conversation, saved reading, or a text/transcript drop. Let the person start with one. Ask what history to include. Read one real item and show what was found so the connection feels concrete.

“When would you like me to check in?” Capture their schedule and timezone, then verify the native scheduler and delivery surface. Explain limits at the relevant moment, such as a seven-day Muse job expiry or a local host that must remain available.

Start now: show one source-grounded observation and ask one optional question. Let a reply deepen the Moment without requiring a title, tags or a taxonomy. Preview the exact upload, including original attribution and any selected reflection. The person can keep, change or dismiss it.

After explicit review and once server integration is available, save privately to the authenticated account, read back the result and open the lane. Ask separately about public display when the person wants it. Private live persistence is the baseline success; public publishing is optional.

## Ongoing check-in

New material produces a small, grounded invitation, not a dump of everything collected. If no new material exists, offer a fresh question or a relevant previous Moment, without inventing something that happened. If a source failed, say so briefly and still offer a way to continue. Leave room for no answer. Do not stack missed prompts or nag until answered.

Suggested product states:

```text
selected source -> gathered locally -> candidate -> human review
                                                   | change -> new proposal
                                                   | dismiss -> retain source, exclude review
                                                   | approve -> private upload -> verified Moment
                                                                                 | approved Snapshot
                                                                                 | chosen publication
```

Upload review must bind the final payload and account. Approval of a short excerpt cannot upload an entire transcript. Raw media, source URLs, speaker identities and machine interpretations must be visible in the review if they are included. Attachments cannot be uploaded first merely to render the review.

## Source connection is part of the product

Show included scope, actual execution location, last success, pending items and recovery. Handle expired authentication with a direct reconnection step. A user's agent subscription is not permission or technical access to every conversation or social account.

Avoid separate integrations for each AI host where standard source tools suffice. Prefer the host's existing authorized connectors/MCP tools; store portable provenance and checkpoints in the local KindHuman adapter. Build bespoke source connectors only where that creates a reliable, valuable path.

## Honest completion

“Installed” means files exist. “Connected” means a real authorized read succeeded. “Scheduled” means the native scheduler confirms registration. “Saved live” means the server accepted the reviewed record for the right account and a read-back/page verified it. The current release completes local installation/capture/review; the rest remains explicitly visible in status.
